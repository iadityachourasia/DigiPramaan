==================================================
1. CORE PRODUCT VISION
==================================================

DigiPramaan is not just a one-time packaged-commodity scanner.

It is intended to become a complete Legal Metrology inspection, compliance, evidence, enforcement-follow-up, product intelligence, company intelligence, and risk-prioritization platform.

The base SIH problem statement requires the system to:

- scan packaged commodities
- extract mandatory declarations
- validate Legal Metrology compliance
- detect missing/non-compliant declarations
- check readability/font-size/placement where feasible
- generate reports
- preserve inspection history
- provide dashboards
- support officers and hierarchy users
- provide search/retrieval
- export PDF/editable reports

DigiPramaan must satisfy all of those core requirements.

However, the product now has FOUR CORE USPs that must be treated as first-class domain requirements:

1. Company Compliance Profile
2. Product Compliance DNA
3. Compliance Follow-Through
4. Smart Risk Alerts

These are not optional future widgets.

8. USP #4 — SMART RISK ALERTS
==================================================

Definition:

A deterministic enforcement-prioritization layer that analyzes verified historical compliance behaviour and identifies entities/products/cases that deserve officer attention.

This is NOT the Legal Metrology Rule Engine.

These must remain separate.

Legal Rule Engine:
determines whether one inspected package complies with applicable law.

Risk Engine:
determines which verified historical patterns should be prioritized for enforcement attention.

Do NOT use an LLM to directly assign risk in the MVP.

Risk logic should be deterministic and explainable.

Potential MVP risk rules:

REPEAT COMPANY VIOLATION
If the same company has the same major/critical rule failure in multiple verified inspections within a defined period.

REPEAT PRODUCT VIOLATION
If the same Product Compliance DNA fails the same rule across multiple batches/inspections.

MULTI-PRODUCT PATTERN
If multiple different products from one company become non-compliant within a time window.

DETERIORATING COMPLIANCE TREND
If recent compliance rate materially deteriorates compared with earlier verified inspections.

UNRESOLVED CASE
If serious follow-through cases remain open.

OVERDUE FOLLOW-UP
If ACTION_REQUIRED / AWAITING_RESPONSE cases exceed their deadline.

RECURRENT MAJOR VIOLATIONS
If the entity repeatedly accumulates major/critical violations.

RISK SCORE must remain separate from COMPLIANCE SCORE.

Compliance Score:
How compliant is this specific inspected package?

Risk Score:
How urgently should enforcement prioritize this historical entity/product/case?

Potential risk dimensions:

- severity of verified violations
- repeat violation frequency
- recency
- unresolved case count
- overdue follow-up
- multi-product spread
- deteriorating trend

Possible score bands:

0–29
LOW

30–59
MODERATE

60–79
HIGH

80–100
CRITICAL

This score should be explicitly described as an enforcement prioritization score, not a legal guilt score.

- subject ID
